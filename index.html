Shut-down companies

Your company closed. Its data can still pay out $100K–$2M.

Years of Slack or Teams history, tickets, and docs are often the last asset left on the books. The proceeds can go back to investors, cover pending dues, or help close the chapter on a better note.

Check if your data qualifies →

You qualify if:

  • Your company had 50+ employees at its peak
  • You still have access to company data or exports
  • Your team worked mainly in English
  • You were based in the US, UK, Canada, India, Australia, Singapore, Ireland, Netherlands, Germany, or France
